face, hair and shirt are 5 16x16 frames: one per variant.
pants and shirtskin are 15 16x16 frames: 5 variants x 3 walk poses, so variant v
sits at frames v, v+5, v+10.

each should render in the same spot, as the relative locations are correct.
the only restriction on rendering is that hair should render on top of player

you can mix and match between all the files, except that shirt and shirtskin
should be one to one (e.g. if shirt is on sprite 1, so should shirtskin)

all sprites can be modulated to any color

scripts/custom_person.gd is the renderer, and web/shell.html reproduces it in
canvas for the character creator -- both need updating if the sheets change.
